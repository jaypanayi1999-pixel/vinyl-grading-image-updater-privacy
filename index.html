# Privacy Policy – Vinyl Grading Image Updater **Last updated: 15 November 2026** ## About this application Vinyl Grading Image Updater is a private application developed to help manage and update product photographs on the owner's eBay selling accounts. ## Information accessed The application may access eBay listing information, including: - Listing IDs - Listing titles - Listing descriptions - Listing photographs The application is designed to manage listing images and does not intentionally access buyer names, addresses, payment details or private messages. ## How information is used Information is used solely to identify relevant vinyl record listings, generate reports and manage listing photographs when authorised by the account owner. Original image URLs and listing image backups may be retained locally to support recovery if required. ## Storage and security Listing reports and backups are stored locally by the application owner. eBay authentication credentials are handled securely and are not published. Cloudflare is used to host the application's notification endpoint. ## Sharing Information accessed by this application is not sold or shared for advertising or marketing purposes. ## Data retention Listing reports and backups are retained only as required for the application's operation. The application owner can delete locally stored reports and backups. ## Contact For privacy enquiries, please contact the owner through the relevant eBay selling account.
